# Roadmap

What Serifu is working on and what comes after. Plans change; dates are not promised. Have an idea that is not
here? [Suggest it](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

## Coming next

- **Seven more languages to translate into:** Japanese, Spanish (Latin America), Portuguese (Brazil), French,
  Italian, German and Indonesian.
- **The app in your language:** Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, German and Indonesian, following your
  computer's language or chosen in Settings.

## Later

- **serifu.net in more languages,** Korean first.
- **Serifu on your phone:** iPhone first, then Android.

## Done recently

- Fonts on this computer: use the fonts installed on your computer, and a default font for each language (1.0.14).
- Webtoons: long strips cut into pages, translated, and exported as one long image (1.0.13).
- Translate in your browser on serifu.net, no install.
- Serifu for Windows.

Every release is in the [changelog](https://serifu.net/changelog).
